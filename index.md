🛡️ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

EasySpot respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how EasySpot collects and uses your information.

📍 Information We Collect

EasySpot collects only the minimum data necessary for its core functionality.

Location Data

EasySpot uses your device’s location to provide its core functionality, including saving parking locations and helping you find your parked vehicle.

The app may access the following types of location data:

• Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to determine your general area
• Precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) to accurately save and retrieve parking locations

Location data is accessed only while the app is actively in use (foreground). EasySpot does not collect or access location data in the background.

All location data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted, shared, sold, or used for advertising or analytics purposes.

Local Storage

EasySpot stores data locally on your device, including:

• Saved parking locations
• Favorite locations

This data persists until you delete it or uninstall the app.

🛠️ How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

• Provide core app functionality
• Improve your experience

We never use your information for advertising, marketing, or analytics purposes.

🔁 Data Sharing

EasySpot does not share, sell, or rent your data to third parties. Your information stays on your device.

🔐 Data Security

EasySpot does not transmit personal data to external servers.

All data is stored locally on your device. While we take steps to protect it, you are responsible for securing access to your device.

🧹 Your Rights

You can:

• Delete saved parking and favorite locations at any time
• Clear app data using in-app controls or your device settings

⚙️ Device Permissions

EasySpot may request access to your device’s location. You can grant or deny this permission and change it at any time in your device settings.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Major changes will appear in the app. Please review it periodically.
